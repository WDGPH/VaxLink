Reading the evidence

Facts: the timeline shows only events the recorder received. A delivered scan is not a verified fill, and a verified fill does not prove a saved clinical record.

Hypotheses: an observed delay or missing stage can guide investigation; it does not identify a slow server or workstation by itself.

Missing evidence: recording may start late, events can be dropped, contexts can restart, and final events may be absent. Page timing is incomplete and excludes other contexts.
