Scanner setup

Use this for an existing USB CDC setup or a generic scanner. The scanner controls its own beep.

Program your Zebra DS8178

Upload any stored scans, then choose Disconnect in VaxLink Settings before programming. Keep the scanner near its powered, paired cradle.

Open the printable setup sheet (PDF). Print at 100% scale and scan one symbol per step.

For an optional clean start, scan Set Factory Defaults first. This clears custom defaults and settings.

  1. Normal (do not batch)
  2. Auto-Reconnect Immediately
  3. SSI over USB CDC. Wait for the connection to restart.
  4. Send Packeted Decode Data
  5. Enable ACK/NAK
  6. Data As Is
  7. Do Not Beep After Good Decode

If your SSI settings were customized, use the Zebra guide to set Multipacket Option 1 (page 9-19) and the 2-second Host Serial Response Timeout (page 9-17).

Connect the scanner, reopen setup and choose Test saved scanner to test the three sounds.

  1. USB CDC Host. Wait for the connection to restart.
  2. Add Enter Key (Carriage Return/Line Feed)
  3. Beep After Good Decode
  4. Enable Pair On Contacts
  5. Disable Auto-Reconnect
  6. Out of Range Batch Mode

Connect with this setup in VaxLink and choose the intended workflow before docking to upload. Review the tray and any held scans after upload.

Closing setup restores the previous capture setting.

Listen for one short high beep, two short low beeps, and three long low beeps.
