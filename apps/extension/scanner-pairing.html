Scanner setup

Use this for an existing USB CDC setup or a generic scanner. The scanner controls its own beep.

Program your Zebra DS8178

Upload any stored scans, then choose Disconnect in VaxLink Settings before programming. Keep the scanner near its powered, paired cradle.

Open the single-scan setup sheet (PDF). Print at 100% scale. Scan only the barcode for the setup you selected.

Each barcode clears custom defaults and settings, then applies the chosen setup. Wait for the USB port to return before connecting. Test on a training workstation while hardware validation is pending.

To reset and program a DS8178, scan Standard USB CDC on page 3. If your scanner already uses USB CDC, choose Connect scanner.

Scan Live feedback on page 1. Connect the scanner, reopen setup and choose Test saved scanner to test the three sounds.

Scan Long range on page 2. Connect the scanner and choose the workflow before docking to upload. Review the tray and any held scans after upload.

Closing setup restores the previous capture setting.

Listen for one short high beep, two short low beeps, and three long low beeps.
